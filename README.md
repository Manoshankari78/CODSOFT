🍽️ Task 3: Restaurant Menu App
This is the third project completed as part of my UI/UX Internship at CodSoft.
The design focuses on creating an intuitive and visually appealing digital restaurant menu to enhance the customer dining experience.

🎨 Features
✅ Clear and consistent menu categories
✅ Visually appealing layout with attractive typography and imagery
✅ Easy navigation for quick browsing and ordering
✅ Designed to encourage customer engagement and upselling
✅ Mobile-friendly design
